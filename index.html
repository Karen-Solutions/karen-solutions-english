MY ENGLISH JOURNEY

Blog Posts

Seven stories, reflections and experiences that are part of my English learning process.

01
Personal Life

Who I Am and Where I Want to Go

Every learning journey begins with a purpose. I am currently studying Software Analysis and Development, and one of my most important goals is to build a professional career in technology.

I see myself working as a software developer, participating in interesting projects and collaborating with people from different countries. For this reason, learning English has become an important part of my personal and professional development.

I know that learning a language takes time, discipline and practice. I do not expect to learn everything immediately. Instead, I want to improve step by step and use every opportunity to communicate more confidently.

02
Hobbies & Interests

Learning Through the Things I Enjoy

Learning does not only happen in a classroom. Music, videos, technology and everyday activities can also become useful learning resources.

I can listen to songs in English, watch videos with subtitles and search for information about topics that interest me. These activities help me discover vocabulary and become more familiar with pronunciation and common expressions.

When learning is connected with something I enjoy, practicing becomes more natural. My objective is to make English part of my daily environment instead of seeing it only as an academic subject.

03
Technology

English and My Future in Software Development

English has an important role in software development. Programming languages use many English words, and a large amount of technical documentation is also written in English.

As an ADSO student, I work with concepts related to programming, databases, interfaces and software requirements. Understanding technical English can help me read documentation, solve problems and learn new technologies more independently.

In the future, I would like to participate in international projects and communicate with developers and clients from different countries. Improving my English brings me closer to that goal.

04
Travel & Communication

English Connects People and Cultures

Traveling shows us how important communication can be. When people do not share the same native language, English can become a useful bridge between them.

Knowing English can make everyday situations easier, such as asking for information, understanding directions, using transportation, ordering food or meeting new people.

Language learning is also cultural learning. Communicating with people from different backgrounds teaches us to listen, adapt and respect different ways of thinking and living.

05
Learning Strategies

Building My Personal Learning Environment

A Personal Learning Environment, or PLE, includes the resources, strategies and tools that a person uses to manage their own learning.

My English learning environment can include educational videos, online resources, dictionaries, artificial intelligence tools, technical documentation and conversations. Each resource can support a different language skill.

I can also organize my learning by setting small goals, practicing frequently and identifying the areas where I need improvement. This allows me to become more responsible for my own progress.

06
Critical Thinking

Critical Thinking and Media Literacy

We receive information every day through social networks, websites, videos and digital media. However, not everything we see online is accurate or reliable.

Critical thinking helps us analyze information before accepting or sharing it. We should ask important questions: Who created this information? What evidence supports it? Is the source reliable? Are other trustworthy sources reporting the same information?

Media literacy is especially important in a world where information can spread very quickly. Responsible digital citizens should verify information, recognize misleading content and understand that algorithms can influence what they see online.

For me, developing these abilities is also useful in software development because technology professionals constantly search for information and make decisions based on digital resources.

07
Society

Crime and Punishment

Crime and punishment are complex topics because societies need to protect people while also respecting rights and legal processes.

Laws establish rules about actions that are considered crimes and the consequences associated with them. However, punishment is not the only aspect of a justice system. Prevention, rehabilitation and social reintegration can also play important roles.

Technology has created new challenges in this area. Cybercrime, identity theft, online fraud and unauthorized access to information demonstrate that crime can also occur in digital environments.

As future technology professionals, we should understand the importance of ethics, privacy and responsible use of information. Technology can be powerful, and using it responsibly is part of our professional responsibility.
